2. **Neutral Interfaces & Deterministic Fakes (`app/speech/`, `app/understanding/`, `app/response/`, `app/vision/`):**
   - **Speech Recognition (`app/speech/`):**
     - `ASRProvider` abstract interface.
     - `FakeASRProvider`: Supports `success`, `abstain`, `timeout`, `malformed`, and `error` modes. Returns versioned `ASRResult` conforming to domain contracts.
   - **Speech Synthesis (`app/speech/`):**
     - `TTSProvider` abstract interface.
     - `FakeTTSProvider`: Supports pre-recorded cache retrieval (`TTSSource.CACHE`) and fallback synthesis (`TTSSource.DYNAMIC`), plus timeout and failure injection.
   - **Intent Understanding (`app/understanding/`):**
     - `SemanticResolver` abstract interface.
     - `FakeSemanticResolver`: Deterministic exact/substring matching and semantic resolving. Strictly enforces `FR-009` invariant: uncertain results are marked `status=UNCERTAIN` and `is_correct=False`, NEVER `is_correct=True`.
   - **Response Planning (`app/response/`):**
     - `Paraphraser` abstract interface.
     - `FakeParaphraser`: Enforces strict brevity constraint of <= 25 words (`AI-008`, `ADR-007`). Supports `passthrough`, `paraphrase`, `timeout`, `oversized`, and `error` modes.
   - **Computer Vision (`app/vision/`):**
     - `VisionProvider` abstract interface.
     - `FakeVisionProvider`: Restricts detections to approved vocabulary (`AI-009`, `ADR-010`), returns normalized bounding boxes `[0.0, 1.0]`, and supports `abstain`, `timeout`, and `error` modes.

3. **Provider Registry & Dependency Injection (`app/providers/registry.py`):**
   - `ProviderRegistry` container with `create_provider_registry(settings)`.
   - Checks feature flags (`cloud_ai_enabled`, `offline_demo_mode`) and raises `ProviderDisabledError` if disabled (`DEV-004`).

4. **Client & Companion Simulators (`simulators/`):**
   - **Hardware Doll Simulator (`simulators/device_simulator.py`):**
     - Simulates ESP32 doll client generating versioned envelopes (`API-001`).
     - Supports `handshake`, `audio_chunk` (enforces 32KB payload size boundary), and `turn_event`.
     - Validates and stores received `DeviceCommand` and `DeviceAck` frames.
   - **Flutter Companion Simulator (`simulators/flutter_simulator.py`):**
     - Ingests `ChildProgressDTO` and `SessionSummaryDTO` payloads (`API-004`).
     - Enforces non-clinical phrasing checks (`SEC-010`), rejecting diagnostic or pathological terminology.

   - Guarantees AI adapters are pure evidence/rendering adapters with zero access to persistence or system tools.

6. **Provider & Simulator Documentation (`docs/providers-and-simulators.md`):**
